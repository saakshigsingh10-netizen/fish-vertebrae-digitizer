Anatomical Landmark Guide
Weberian Apparatus ($W$)

Anterior modified vertebrae (usually 4 in otophysans like Astyanax) connecting swim bladder to inner ear. Labelled as $W_1$ to $W_4$ and typically excluded from standard vertebra counts.

Precaudal Vertebrae ($Pre$)

Rib-bearing abdominal vertebrae ($1 \dots n$). Numbered consecutively starting immediately after the Weberian apparatus.

Caudal Vertebrae ($Cau$)

Vertebrae possessing a closed ventral haemal arch and haemal spine starting at $C_1$.

Fused Vertebrae & Urostyle ($U$)

Fused centra (e.g. vertebrae 18-19 sharing a single combined centrum body with bifurcated haemal spines) count as individual units in total counts. The terminal Urostyle ($U$) is excluded.
