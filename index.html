Vertebral Differentiation Criteria
Precaudal (Abdominal) Vertebrae ($P_1 .. P_n$)

Anterior body vertebrae located above the visceral/abdominal cavity. They bear pleural ribs or ventral parapophyses. Do NOT possess a closed haemal arch.

Caudal Vertebrae ($C_1 .. C_m$)

Posterior vertebrae starting immediately after the abdominal cavity ($C_1$). They are defined by possessing a fully closed ventral haemal arch enclosing the caudal blood vessels and terminating in a haemal spine.

How $C_1$ is AI-Identified

AI scans for the radiolucent boundary of the swim bladder/abdominal cavity. The first centrum posterior to the abdominal wall with a descending ventral haemal arch is classified as $C_1$.
