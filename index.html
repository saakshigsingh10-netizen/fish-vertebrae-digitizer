Otophysan Anatomical Guide
Weberian Apparatus (W₁ - W₄)
Anterior 4 modified vertebrae in Otophysi (Cypriniformes, Characiformes, Siluriformes, Gymnotiformes). $V_1$ and $V_2$ are shortened; $V_3$ and $V_4$ bear the tripus and os suspensorium connecting the swim bladder to the inner ear. Ribs begin on $V_5$ ($P_1$).
Precaudal Vertebrae (P₁ - Pₙ)
Abdominal centra starting at $V_5$ in Otophysans, bearing paired pleural ribs extending into the body wall.
Caudal Vertebrae (C₁ - Cₙ)
Posterior centra featuring fully closed ventral haemal arches and spines.
