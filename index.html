Anatomical Landmarks
1. Weberian Apparatus (W)

First 4 anterior vertebrae (V1-V4) behind skull. Highly modified, lacking normal ribs.

2. Precaudal Vertebrae (Pre)

Rib-bearing abdominal vertebrae (typically Pre 1-12 in A. mexicanus).

3. Caudal Vertebrae (Cau)

Hemal spine-bearing vertebrae extending to the tail base (typically Cau 13-30).

4. Urostyle (U)

Terminal upward-tilted fused centrum supporting caudal fin rays.
